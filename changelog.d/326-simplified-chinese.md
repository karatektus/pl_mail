**The interface is available in Simplified Chinese.** Choose **简体中文** during setup or under
Settings → General → Language. Mail, calendar, settings, administration, sign-in errors and
validation messages are translated, and so is the emoji picker's search. Following this language
uses a 24-hour clock; an explicitly chosen clock format still takes precedence. No schema change.
[#326](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/326), by @CorrectRoadH.
