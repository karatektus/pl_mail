**Upload a logo of your own.** Settings → Appearance → Logo has an **Upload your own…** button
that takes an SVG and wears it at once: in the top bar, as the tab icon, and as the icon of the
installed app and the iPhone Home Screen, which your browser draws from it as it does for the
built-in icons. It sits beside them as a **Your own** tile, so you can switch away and back, and
it can be replaced or removed. The file is cleaned before it is kept, so nothing in it can run or
load anything. The phone apps keep showing your previous icon. Schema change: two new appearance
columns. [#48](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/48), requested by @Apollosport.
