**Choose which folders wait behind More in the sidebar.** Settings → General → Sidebar lists Unread,
Starred, Sent, Drafts, Snoozed, Archive, Spam and Trash with a box each: ticked ones sit behind
**More**, cleared ones stay in the sidebar itself. Nothing moves until you use it — Snoozed, Archive,
Spam and Trash stay behind More, as before. The Inbox always stays on top, and with nothing ticked
there is no More at all. It only decides where a row sits: the eye in Settings → Labels still hides
one altogether. The choice is kept in a configuration backup, and the narrow icon rail, which has no
More, shows only the folders on top. No migration.
[#151](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/151), by @Flouss.

**Settings save without reloading the page.** A switch, box or choice in Settings used to reload the
whole page and drop you back at the top of it. The page now updates in place: the card you changed,
its confirmation and the sidebar beside it change where they are, and you stay where you were
scrolled. The language and the 12- or 24-hour clock still reload the page, because they change
everything on it. No migration.
