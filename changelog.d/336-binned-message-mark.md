**A message in the bin is marked as such inside a conversation that is not.** A conversation stays
in the Inbox and its labels until every message of it is deleted, so opening one that was only
partly deleted showed the deleted messages among the live ones with nothing to tell them apart. A
deleted message now carries an "In Trash" chip and a dimmed header; in Trash itself, where every
message is deleted, nothing is marked. The message's own Archive and Delete entries were already
hidden for mail in the bin. No schema change.
