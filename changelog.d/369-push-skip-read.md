**Browser notifications can be limited to the inbox tabs you choose, and skip mail you have already
read.** With web push on in more than one browser, every browser said "New mail" for every message
that reached the Inbox, whatever its tab, and kept doing so after the message was read or deleted on
a phone. Settings → Notifications has two new choices, for your whole account rather than one
device. **Notify for** has a box for each tab (Primary, Social, Promotions, Updates, Forums), all
ticked as before; plMail decides on the server, and mail the assistant holds back is announced once
it has been put in a tab you ticked. **Only notify for unread mail** makes a browser wait twenty
seconds after the push and show nothing if the Inbox has nothing unread by then, taking down a
notification it had shown for earlier mail. Both leave things as they were until you change them.
The Android app already narrows to Primary by itself and is not affected. No schema change.
