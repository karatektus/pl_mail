**Pasting into a mail keeps its formatting and its line spacing (#807).** Formatting that the
source app set with CSS was lost on paste: a Google Docs paste turned entirely bold and lost its
italics, and Pages and TextEdit pastes lost bold and italic altogether. Lines that sat directly
under each other came out with a paragraph's gap between them. Pastes now keep bold, italic,
underline, colour, highlighting, font and size, and keep the source's line spacing, while the
source page's layout is still left out. When an app also puts a picture of the copied text on the
clipboard, the text is pasted, not the picture. A new **Paste as plain text** button in the
formatting bar pastes only the words, for phones, which have no Shift-paste.

**The compose window opens wider, so its formatting bar fits on one line (#807).** At its old
standard width the bar broke onto a second line. It now opens 760 pixels wide where the screen has
room for that. A window you have resized keeps your size.
