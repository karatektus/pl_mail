**The insight strip above the mail list keeps up without a reload.** A strip that was empty when the
page loaded never noticed new insights, so a parcel or a flight found after that stayed hidden until
the next reload. And once the strip was waved away with ✕, or its last card dismissed, nothing new
could bring it back until a reload, although a newer insight is meant to. Both now appear as soon
as the sync finds them, including across workspace switches. No schema change.
