**Browser notifications can name the sender and the subject.** Switch on **Settings → Notifications
→ Show sender and subject** and a notification for new mail says who wrote and the subject instead
of "New mail", and a click opens that conversation. Mail that came in together is one notification,
"3 new messages", with the senders. A click on a notification now uses the plMail tab that is open,
as Gmail does, instead of opening another. The push itself still carries nothing about the mail: the
browser asks plMail for the words over your own session when it arrives. Off by default, because the
words then show on a locked screen too. No schema change.
[#760](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/760), by @swell-d.
