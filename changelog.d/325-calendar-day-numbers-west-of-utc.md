**Calendar dates are right west of UTC.** Anywhere in the Americas, a day or week heading showed
the day before beside the right weekday — Friday the 6th read "FRI 5" — and the month view
numbered every cell a day early, shaded Friday and Saturday as the weekend and dimmed the 1st as
part of the previous month. Calendars east of UTC were not affected. No schema change.
