**The mail list updates when mail changes elsewhere, not only the counts beside it (#807).** The
badges in the sidebar and on the inbox tabs already followed every change to your mail, but the
list only re-read itself after a sync. So when the assistant moved a new mail to another inbox tab
after it had been shown, or a message was read or archived on your phone or in another tab, a tab
could say "1" over a list without that mail until you clicked it. The list now follows the same
changes. Your own clicks in the same tab don't make it reload.
