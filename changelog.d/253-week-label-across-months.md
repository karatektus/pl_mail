**A week that runs into the next month is no longer cut off at the top of the calendar on a small
phone.** On a 320px screen the week's label had room for "Oct 26–N…", and a week across New Year
for even less. There it now names the two months ("Oct–Nov"); the days are in the header right
below. No schema change. [#253](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/253), by
@pmdevelopment.
