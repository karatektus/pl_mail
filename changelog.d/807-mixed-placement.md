**An Inbox conversation always shows Archive, Delete and Snooze when you open it (#807).** If an
earlier message in the conversation had been archived or marked as spam, the reading pane counted
the whole conversation as archived or spam and hid those buttons, even with the newest mail in the
Inbox. That happened most often with Gmail, which puts a reply into the old conversation, so it
seemed to depend on who wrote. A conversation with mail in the Inbox now counts as Inbox. Opened
from Spam, it still offers what Spam offers.
