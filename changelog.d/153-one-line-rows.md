**One preview line now means one line per message.** With *Preview lines* set to *One line* (the
default), a mail list at least 48rem wide shows sender, subject and preview on a single, slightly
tighter line, the preview following the subject as in Gmail. Until now that took a list 75rem wide;
narrower lists put the preview on a line of its own. *None* and *Two lines* are unchanged, and so
is the list on a phone.
