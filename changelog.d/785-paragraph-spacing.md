**Sent mail is spaced the way it was written.** The composer makes a paragraph of every line and shows
it without a gap, but the mail went out with plain paragraphs, which mail programs draw with a blank
line's worth of space above and below: every line arrived with a blank line after it, and a blank line
as two, in plMail's own reading pane too. Each paragraph you write now goes out with no margin, as the
composer shows it; the quote under a reply stays as the sender wrote it. Mail sent before is not
changed. No schema change. [#785](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/785), reported by
@swell-d.
