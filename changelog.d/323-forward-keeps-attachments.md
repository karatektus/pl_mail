**A forward carries the original's attachments.** Forwarding a message sent only the quoted text;
its files stayed behind. The forward now opens with them attached, removable like any other, within
the usual 25 MB. Pictures that are part of the body stay in the quote, and a signed or encrypted
mail's signature or ciphertext is not attached. Replies are unchanged. No schema change.
[#323](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/323), by @phdelodder.
