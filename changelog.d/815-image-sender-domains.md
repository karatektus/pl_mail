**Remote images: trust a whole domain or a pattern, and see the whole list (#815).** Some senders
use a new address for every mail, so "Always show images from" one of them never covered the next.
The images bar now also offers "Always show images from everyone at" the sender's domain, except at
providers like gmail.com where anybody can have an address. Settings → Reading & writing → Reading
lists every trusted sender with a remove button each. Its field adds addresses, whole domains
(`*@example.com`) and patterns such as `invoice+*@stripe.com`, several at once.
