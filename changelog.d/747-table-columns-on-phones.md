**Tables in mail keep whole words on a phone.** A table with fixed-width columns beside a free
one, such as Bitbucket's commit notifications, squeezed the free column to one letter a line on a
narrow screen, because the reading frame let words break between any two letters. Long words and
links still wrap; a table that is wider than the screen now scrolls sideways, as in Gmail. No schema
change. [#747](https://github.com/karatektus/pl_mail/issues/747), reported by @dzonder.
