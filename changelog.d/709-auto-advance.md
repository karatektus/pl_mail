**The next conversation can open by itself after you archive the one you are reading.** A new
choice under Settings → General → Layout & controls, Auto-advance, opens the conversation below or
above in the list after you archive, delete, move, snooze or report the open one, instead of going
back to the list. Going back stays the default. No schema change.
