**On a phone, the category tabs fit on the screen.** Primary, Social, Promotions and the others
were wider than a phone, so most of the row sat past the edge and a tab you could not see could not
tell you it held unread mail. On a narrow screen only the tab you are on now shows its name; the
others keep their icon, their unread count and their "new" pill, and four of them fit on a
phone without scrolling. The row still scrolls sideways when there are more tabs than fit. On wider
screens the tabs are a little closer together. No schema change.
